Notable Hardware revision changes for Olimex ESP32-POE2 board

Hardware revision B2
===
1. FET1 , FET2, FET3 changed to IRLML6402TRPbF;
2. Changed a couple of capacitors due to customer feedback problems:
	c16 changed to 100uF/100V/20%/10x12MM/Ymin;
	c22 changed to 220uF/50V/20%/8*12MM/Ymin;
	c29 changed to 470uF/16V/20%/8*12MM/Ymin.

Hardware revision B1
===
1. Changed the Li-PO charger with TP4054-42-SOT25-R(SOT23-5).

Revision B
===
Initial release
